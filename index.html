Tropical storm Isaias forms, NHC forecasts hurricane prior to Gulf Coast landfall

Artemis, citing the National Hurricane Center forecast · Oct 7 · forecast, subject to change
The NHC forecasts Isaias will strengthen to a Category 2 hurricane, with peak winds around 90–95 knots, before a Gulf Coast landfall. The forecast track runs from central Louisiana to the western Florida Panhandle, centered near Gulfport, Mississippi, though dry air and wind shear could change its final strength. Artemis says a Category 2 landfall would typically not affect catastrophe bonds but would matter for primary insurers and some reinsurance.

Workers' comp's pricing correction has a patient zero: California

Insurance Business America, citing WCIRB and NCCI data and a Safety National executive · Oct 7
California workers' comp rates in Q1 2026 were about 6% higher than 2025 levels, reversing a decade-long decline. The state's 2025 accident-year combined ratio reached 127%, compared with 102% nationally. Cumulative trauma claims rose to 31.6% of California indemnity claims in 2025, from 26.6% in 2024.

Hackers Breached Propulsion System of US-Bound Oil Tanker

Insurance Journal, citing Bloomberg News · Oct 5 · investigation findings reported
FBI and Coast Guard investigators found that hackers gained access to the propulsion system of the supertanker VL Prosperity as it approached Texas. It is a rare case of a cyberattack reaching a ship's operational systems rather than its business IT.

National Flood Insurance Program's Authority to Expire in December

Insurance Journal · Oct 7 · current authority lapses Dec. 12, 2026
The NFIP's authority is set to lapse on Dec. 12, 2026, under its 36th short-term extension since fiscal 2017 ended. The program covers nearly 4.5 million policies and about $1.3 trillion in coverage. Industry groups continue to push for long-term reauthorization and reform of flood mapping and rating.

Insurers allege U.S. Bank wired $14M, ticked its own approval box

Insurance Business America · Oct 7 · complaint filed Oct. 5; allegations, no ruling
United Specialty Insurance Company and State National Insurance Company sued U.S. Bank in federal court in the Northern District of Texas. They allege the bank, as trustee, wired $14.08 million out of reinsurance trust accounts in August 2025 without the required beneficiary approval and then marked its own checklists as if approval had been received. The insurers say they have booked more than $200 million in expected credit losses tied to the broader reinsurance program.

Cat bond risk spreads fall 9.5% in September, but rising risk free rate helps to offset: Plenum

Artemis, citing Plenum Investments data (fund manager) · Oct 7
Catastrophe bond risk spreads fell 9.5% in September, to 4.57% by Sept. 25 from 5.05% in late August. Higher US Treasury yields lifted collateral returns from 3.81% to 4.17%, keeping overall cat bond coupon yields roughly steady at 8.74%.

TWIA forecasts $2.05bn reinsurance / cat bond need for 2027, proposes 18% budget reduction

Artemis · Oct 7 · staff proposal, pending board approval
The Texas Windstorm Insurance Association expects to need about $2.05 billion of reinsurance and cat bond protection for 2027, roughly 10% less than in 2026. Staff propose a risk-transfer budget of about $173 million, nearly 18% below 2026's almost $210 million, citing a larger catastrophe reserve fund and softer pricing.

FTC, USDA Seek Comment on Farm Equipment Market Practices

Insurance Journal, citing Bloomberg · Oct 7 · public inquiry, not an enforcement action
The FTC and USDA opened a public inquiry into how farm equipment is made, sold and repaired, citing a growing number of complaints from farmers. It includes a look at potential anticompetitive conduct and follows a July settlement with Deere over repair restrictions. Shares of Deere, AGCO, CNH Industrial and Caterpillar fell after the announcement.

Insurance Business America · Oct 7 · gang's claim, not verified
The Everest ransomware group listed UAE airline FlyDubai on its leak site and claims to hold 4.36GB of data, including Boeing source code from an engineering tool. No samples had been released to verify the Boeing claim, and neither company had commented publicly.

Managing Agent-to-Agent AI Risk in the Supply Chain

Risk Management Magazine (RIMS), by a PwC technology compliance manager · Oct 6 · practitioner view
The article looks at risks when AI agents at different companies transact directly with each other without human review. It names three failure points: unclear agent identity, actions that cascade without shared safeguards, and split audit trails. It recommends mutual agent authentication, escalation rules for cross-company actions, standard logging and partner governance reviews.

Comp industry looks beyond AI time savings to worker outcomes

Business Insurance, citing Rising Medical Solutions' 2026 benchmarking study (vendor survey) · Oct 7
In the study, 58% of workers' comp claims respondents said AI had improved staff efficiency, but 35% had not assessed its effect on claim closure. Industry leaders quoted said AI should support adjusters' judgment and engagement with injured workers rather than just raise caseloads.

Blended cyber, tech E&O coverage requires careful assessment

Business Insurance, quoting Canopius, Howden, Lockton Re and Saul Ewing panelists · Oct 7 · conference panel views
Panelists at the NetDiligence Cyber Risk Summit debated whether to combine cyber liability and technology errors and omissions cover in one policy. They said buyers should weigh their risk tolerance, capital and coverage needs, since a blended policy brings trade-offs such as shared limits.

El Niño is reshaping global catastrophe risk: Moody's analysis

Insurance Business America, citing Moody's analysis (rating agency / modeler view) · Oct 7
Moody's says this year's El Niño has suppressed Atlantic hurricanes, with none recorded as of its early-October analysis, while the Eastern Pacific has had ten. It warns that a quiet Atlantic season is not a safe one, since a single storm can cause losses above $50 billion. It also flags higher Australian bushfire risk and shifting Asian monsoon patterns.

Europe split on insurance strategy

CIR Magazine, citing WBN's New Risk Reality Report with MarshBerry (broker-network survey) · Oct 7
A survey of 1,000 risk professionals in the UK, France, Germany and Spain named cyber, AI and economic volatility as top concerns. In the UK, 51% see insurance as an investment in resilience, versus 29% in Germany. Rising premiums were the leading insurance concern in every market, cited by 37% to 48% depending on the country.
