Three states have now enacted litigation-funding disclosure laws — and Arizona added a court rule

Lockton · enacted at state level (federal bill still only introduced)
Since the start of 2025, Georgia, Mississippi and Tennessee have each enacted legislation requiring disclosure of funding arrangements or funder identities. Separately, the Arizona Supreme Court amended the state's rules of civil procedure to require parties to disclose a third-party funder's identity, financial terms and degree of strategic control at the outset of a case. Why it matters: status precision matters — the federal S.3826 is still sitting in the Senate Judiciary Committee, but in these four jurisdictions disclosure is already the rule.

2025 tally: 149 nuclear verdicts worth $25.1bn across 28 states

CaseGlide, citing Litigation Sentinel tracking · 2025 full-year data · still relevant
A nuclear verdict is defined as $10M or more, thermonuclear as $100M or more; the 2025 count came to 149 verdicts totalling $25.1bn across 28 states. Why it matters: this updates the 2024 figures carried in yesterday's edition with a full-year 2025 read. The count is spread across more than half the states — not a venue problem that can be avoided by footprint alone.
