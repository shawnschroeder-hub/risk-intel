New Jersey bill on litigation-funding disclosure advances out of committee

Shore News Network · advanced from committee — not yet passed
Assembly Bill 2159 was voted out of the Assembly Financial Institutions and Insurance Committee. It would require disclosure of litigation funding agreements in civil and administrative cases, prohibit funders from influencing legal strategy or settlement decisions, and make violating agreements unenforceable while exposing funders to consumer fraud penalties. Why it matters: New Jersey would be a materially larger jurisdiction than the three states that have already enacted disclosure. Status matters — this cleared one committee, nothing more.

Why it matters: included so the New Jersey item is read in proportion — the states keep moving while the federal bill sits still. Nothing has changed federally since we first covered it.

PFAS exclusions are now standard ISO endorsements on GL, BOP and umbrella forms

Inszone Insurance (broker guidance) · no date shown on source page
ISO specimen forms CG 40 32 05 23 (general liability) and CU 34 54 (umbrella/excess) specifically exclude perfluoroalkyl and polyfluoroalkyl substances, and now appear on CGL, Business Owners and umbrella forms — removing ambiguity and shifting risk back to the insured. Context: PFAS litigation has already produced more than $11bn in water-system settlements, with recent action reaching secondary, textile and cosmetics manufacturers. Why it matters: the most concrete action item today — ask the broker directly whether either endorsement appears on renewal quotes. Note this is broker-published guidance and undated; verify form editions against actual policies.
