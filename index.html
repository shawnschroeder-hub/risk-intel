Binance $6.3 Billion Heist to Prompt Closer Scrutiny by Insurers

Insurance Journal (Reuters) · Nov 6 · development of a story carried Nov 4; broker and lawyer views
Brokers at Gallagher and Marsh expect underwriters to ask crypto clients about their exposure to Binance and similar security weaknesses, mostly at renewal, though Marsh does not expect major changes to capacity, pricing or coverage. Lawyers quoted expect tighter wordings and clearer exclusions, and Lloyd’s cyber policies have generally carried state-backed attack exclusions since 2023. Morningstar DBRS said insurers may dispute claims if an attack is attributed to a state, noting such exclusions are untested in a major loss.

Berkshire Hathaway Boosts Buybacks as Operating Income Slips

Insurance Journal · Nov 9 · company results
Berkshire Hathaway’s third-quarter operating earnings fell 5.8% to $12.7 billion, and insurance underwriting profit dropped 43%, mainly on higher losses and a surge in underwriting expenses at GEICO. Insurance investment income fell 14% to $2.7 billion on lower short-term rates, and Berkshire reported no significant catastrophe events in the quarter. It repurchased about $9.6 billion of its own shares.

Skyward turns away cheap E&S property as its fee engine picks up the slack

Insurance Business America · Nov 9 · company results; industry data from stamping offices
Skyward’s E&S property submissions rose 60% in the third quarter, but its hit ratio fell by a third and its average premium dropped about 20%. Stamping office data cited in the article show E&S property premium fell 13.7% in the first half of 2026 even as transactions rose 15.2%. Skyward’s E&S excess liability submissions rose about 30%, and its combined ratio was 89.9%.

Floods expected to cause hundreds of millions in losses

Business Insurance, citing an Aon catastrophe report (broker) · Nov 6 · early estimate
Aon says early-November flooding in Arizona and Colorado, the Florida peninsula and the southern Appalachians is likely to cause economic and insured losses in the hundreds of millions of dollars, possibly higher. Southern Brevard County, Florida, recorded more than 24 inches of rain in about a day, and parts of north Georgia saw 5 to 11 inches. Aon expects the insured share to be a fraction of the total because many affected owners outside designated flood zones lack flood cover.

Late notice doesn’t doom asbestos coverage claim

Business Insurance · Nov 6 · 3rd Circuit, New Jersey law; remanded, not final
Lab equipment maker GEA Mechanical Equipment US notified three Hartford units about two months after a $70.1 million mesothelioma verdict, and a district court twice ruled for the insurers on late notice. A unanimous Third Circuit panel vacated that ruling, holding that under New Jersey law an insurer must show a likelihood of appreciable prejudice and that “mere conjecture or suspicions” are not enough. The case returns to the district court a second time.

Google AI in School Triggered Teen’s Mental Health Crisis, Lawsuit Says

Insurance Journal (Bloomberg) · Nov 6 · complaint filed, allegations only
Parents of a 13-year-old sued Google in California state court, alleging its Gemini AI on school Chromebooks discussed suicide and self-harm with their daughter for months without warning to parents or schools. The complaint alleges Google marketed Chromebooks as safe while adding AI it knew carried risks, and cites research that a newer Gemini model responded appropriately to mental-health messages 7% of the time. No ruling has been reported, and Google did not immediately comment.

Investors settle with KPMG over Silicon Valley Bank collapse

Business Insurance · Nov 6 · settlement reached; court approval not reported
Investors in a class action over the 2023 collapse of Silicon Valley Bank reached a $224.5 million settlement with KPMG, the bank’s external auditor, according to Norway’s sovereign wealth fund, an investor in the case. KPMG said the settlement is not an admission of wrongdoing. The class action continues against other defendants.

Hannover Re P&C Re operating profit +20.2% despite pricing. EUR 29m losses shared with ILS

Artemis · Nov 9 · nine-month results
Hannover Re’s property and casualty reinsurance operating profit rose 20.2% to EUR 2.2 billion for the first nine months, with a combined ratio of 83.7% as large losses of EUR 1.316 billion came in well below budget. Gross P&C revenue fell 4.9%, and the new-business contract service margin fell 11.9%, which the company partly attributed to price and volume changes at renewals. It has shared EUR 29 million of 2026 losses with insurance-linked securities partners.

ICE to launch European wind futures in weather derivatives expansion

Artemis · Nov 9 · planned Dec 14 launch, subject to regulatory processes
Intercontinental Exchange plans to launch 15 cash-settled wind futures on December 14, based on wind power output indices for seven European countries that ICE says hold over 70% of Europe’s installed wind capacity. The contracts are meant to let wind producers, utilities and power-purchase-agreement buyers hedge wind generation volume and electricity price risk. CME Group also plans wind power futures and options later this year.

Severe Space Weather Could Cost Trillions and Challenges Traditional Insurance

Risk & Insurance, citing an Acrisure Re paper with ETH Zurich (reinsurance broker) · Nov 6 · modelled scenarios, not a forecast
An Acrisure Re paper produced with ETH Zurich estimates that a Carrington-class solar storm could cause global losses from hundreds of billions to several trillion dollars, mainly through prolonged power grid outages, with an estimated recurrence of roughly 80 to 420 years. It cites estimates that a full US GPS outage would cost about $1 billion a day. The paper recommends that insurers test a 1-in-200-year space weather scenario.

Manufacturers Hammer IBHS Over 15-Year Roof Age Limit for Fortified Designation

Insurance Journal · Nov 9 · trade-body criticism; IBHS could not be reached
The Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association is criticizing an IBHS bulletin that, from January 1, 2027, limits Fortified roof recertification to roofs under 15 years old, saying the underlying study does not reflect real-world durability. The bulletin says the rule applies only to Fortified recertification and does not set a service life for asphalt shingles. IBHS reported in May that more than 100,000 structures hold the Fortified designation.

Firms increasingly wary of cyber and AI supply chain risks

CIR Magazine, citing the UK Office for National Statistics business survey · Nov 9 · UK firms with 10+ employees
The ONS October survey found 21% of UK businesses with 10 or more employees concerned about cyber attacks affecting their supply chains over the next 12 months, up 9 points on a year earlier, while 12% cited AI-related disruption. Geopolitical instability was the most-cited supply-chain concern at 31%. Among firms reporting supply-chain concerns, 64% expected higher sourcing costs and 60% higher transport costs.
