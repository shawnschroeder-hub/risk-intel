AI advances coincide with sharp rise in cyber incidents, Marsh data shows

Insurance Journal, via Reuters, citing Marsh (broker; client-reported incident counts) and GlobalData (data and analytics firm; premium forecasts) · Nov 17
Marsh says its clients reported 105 cyber incidents in the last two weeks of October, more than double the 48 recorded in the last two weeks of May. Marsh’s Meredith Schnur points to the speed and scale of AI tool adoption and to organizations taking longer to contain attacks. GlobalData expects global cyber insurance premiums to rise 6.9% to $16.6 billion in 2026 and 10% to $18.3 billion in 2027.

Trump seeks $50 billion for airspace upgrades after outage

Insurance Journal, via Bloomberg · Nov 18 · funding request, not enacted; new development since the Nov 16 and 17 editions
The Trump administration formally asked Congress for $50.1 billion to overhaul the US airspace system, in a letter to House and Senate appropriations leaders, after the Nov. 13 nationwide ground stop. Senate Majority Leader John Thune said lawmakers need more information, noting that about $8 billion already provided to the FAA has not been used. Officials have not given a precise root cause for the router malfunction at an FAA facility in Arkansas, and the FAA on Tuesday halted installations of Hewlett Packard Enterprise Juniper routers possibly linked to the outage.

US cyber security agency warns of urgent cyber risk over software flaw

Insurance Business America · Nov 17 (updated Nov 18) · government alert; no confirmed exploitation reported
CISA on Nov. 17 urged organizations to immediately patch CVE-2026-114938, a critical Oracle Java flaw nicknamed “Java4Shell” that could allow unauthenticated remote code execution. Oracle fixed it in its November 2026 Critical Patch Update, and CISA told organizations to check for Java runtimes bundled in third-party software and to patch internet-facing systems first. The article says the flaw was not in CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue and no confirmed exploitation had been reported, though a proof of concept is public and a second researcher reported building a working exploit.

Data center values outstrip insurance capacity as AI build-out accelerates

Insurance Business America, citing LGT Capital Partners (asset manager) · Nov 18 · uninsured-share figure is an LGT estimate
AI-driven hyperscale data center sites are valued at $30 billion to $45 billion or more, while the conventional property market’s effective single-risk limit generally tops out at $5 billion to $10 billion. LGT Capital Partners, citing market estimates, says one-third to half of data center value is currently uninsured. Marsh launched a facility offering up to $10 billion of single-placement capacity in August 2026, and Aon expanded its data center program to $5 billion in July 2026.

Insurer says eBay not covered for $55.7M settlement for harassing couple

Claims Journal (Insurance Journal’s sister publication) · Nov 18 · insurer’s allegations in a federal filing; no ruling
ACE Property & Casualty is asking a federal court in California to declare that it has no obligation to indemnify eBay or its executives and employees over the settlement with a couple harassed by eBay security staff in 2018 and 2019. ACE says any covered losses were expected or intended, alleges eBay settled without its consent and without allocating among claims, and cites California’s bar on insuring deliberate misconduct. The ACE policy is a $50 million excess umbrella sitting above a Zurich American policy.

Nvidia’s insurance protects board but not company coffers from litigation

Claims Journal, via Reuters, citing The Insurer · Nov 18 · lawsuit allegations, not findings; new coverage details since the Nov 10–12 editions
Reuters, citing The Insurer, reports that Nvidia has up to $950 million of directors and officers coverage but no insurance for the company’s own costs in securities litigation. At least two lawsuits have been filed since Nov. 8, a proposed securities fraud class action in San Francisco and a derivative suit against the directors in San Jose. Nvidia has more than $50 billion in cash, from which any successful securities fraud claims against the company would be paid.

Texas Farm Bureau secures $400m Fish Pond Re 2026-1 cat bond at reduced pricing

Artemis · Nov 18 · priced; settlement expected next month
Texas Farm Bureau Insurance Companies secured $400 million of named storm reinsurance through the Fish Pond Re 2026-1 cat bond, upsized from an initial $250 million target. The $250 million Class A notes priced at 2%, against initial guidance of 2.25% to 2.75%, and the $150 million Class B notes at 2.75%, against 3% to 3.5%. Cover is fully collateralized, on an indemnity trigger, and runs through the end of 2030.

The Hartford seeks lower pricing for $280m Foundation Re IV 2027-1 cat bond

Artemis · Nov 17 · deal still in marketing, not yet priced
The Hartford is still seeking $280 million of US named storm and earthquake protection through the Foundation Re IV 2027-1 cat bond, with price guidance lowered to 2.75% to 3% from 3% to 3.5%. The single-tranche deal has an initial expected loss of 1.56%, an indemnity trigger and a term running to the end of 2030. It effectively renews a $200 million Foundation Re IV 2023-1 layer that matures around year-end.

Texas powder coating company faces $1.6M in proposed OSHA penalties

Business Insurance · Nov 17 · proposed penalties; company may contest
OSHA proposed $1,640,888 in penalties against Houston-based Outdoor Furniture Refinishing Inc., doing business as Allied Powder Coating, for 40 violations found in three inspections on April 10. They include three willful citations for worker exposure to hazardous levels of 1,3,5-triglycidyl isocyanurate and dust, plus 18 serious and 18 repeat citations. OSHA says the company failed to correct recurring hazards despite inspections dating to 2013 and a December 2024 settlement agreement.

Florida approves 7.4% workers comp rate decrease

Business Insurance · Nov 17 · regulatory approval; effective Jan 1, 2027
Florida Insurance Commissioner Mike Yaworsky approved a 7.4% workers compensation rate decrease, described as the state’s 11th consecutive annual rate reduction. The National Council on Compensation Insurance proposed it, and the Office of Insurance Regulation issued a final order after a public hearing. It applies to new and renewal policies from January 1, 2027.

Merger could intensify reinsurance competition: A.M. Best

Business Insurance, citing A.M. Best (rating agency), as covered by (Re)in Asia · Nov 17 · rating-agency view
A.M. Best says the planned April 2027 merger of Mitsui Sumitomo Insurance and Aioi Nissay Dowa Insurance could reduce reinsurance demand and increase competition, with effects that could spread across the Asia-Pacific reinsurance market.
