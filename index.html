L'Oreal explores unloading chemical-related liabilities, WSJ reports

Insurance Journal, citing The Wall Street Journal · Oct 6 · exploring options, no deal announced
L'Oreal's US unit has hired Weil Gotshal & Manges and Ducera Partners to explore options for rising talc and chemical-ingredient liabilities, according to the WSJ report. It faced about 760 pending US proceedings alleging asbestos in talc products as of June 30, up from 620 at the end of 2025. One option is moving the tort liabilities into a separate entity for sale to an outside investor, similar to Honeywell's divestiture of asbestos liabilities to Delticus, outside Chapter 11.

Warren, Hawley press insurers over closed, unpaid claims

Insurance Journal · Oct 6 · senators' information request, not legislation
Senators Elizabeth Warren and Josh Hawley wrote on Oct. 2 to State Farm, Allstate, USAA, Farmers, Liberty Mutual and American Family seeking a decade of data on homeowners and auto claims closed without payment. They cited a Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas finding that homeowners premiums rose 70% from 2019 to 2025. NAMIC and the Insurance Information Institute say the underlying analysis conflated denials with claims closed for reasons such as being under the deductible, withdrawn, or excluded.

How property insurance softness is spilling into workers' comp competition

Insurance Business America, quoting Zurich and Alera Group executives (insurer and broker views) · Oct 5
Workers' comp premiums fell 3.2% in Q2 2026, the 18th consecutive quarterly decline, while commercial property fell 6.3%. Executives quoted say carriers losing property revenue may be pricing comp aggressively to keep growing. Private carriers posted a 91% calendar-year combined ratio in 2025, but the accident-year figure reached 102% as rising severity offset lower frequency.

Insurers fight claims tied to 'tax scam' investments

Business Insurance · Oct 6 · litigation pending
Insurers including Liberty Mutual are defending about 20 lawsuits involving more than $1 billion in coverage limits tied to syndicated conservation easement deals, which the IRS treats as tax-avoidance schemes. The IRS has disallowed more than $36 billion in deductions across 28,000 investors. In one case, a federal judge in Georgia in April denied insurers' motion to compel arbitration, letting it proceed in state court.

High court won't hear insurer's bid to recover $11M from former lease owners

Business Insurance · Oct 6 · cert denied; Fifth Circuit ruling stands
The U.S. Supreme Court declined to hear surety Lexon Insurance's appeal seeking $11.2 million from Chevron and other former owners of an offshore Louisiana oil lease. Lexon paid forfeited decommissioning bonds after leaseholder Linder Oil went bankrupt in 2017. The Fifth Circuit had held that federal law lets a surety sue only "under the bond" and rejected Lexon's unjust enrichment claim.

Economics of ILS and appetites of investors change with the reinsurance cycle: Moody's

Artemis, citing Moody's Ratings analysis (rating agency view) · Oct 6
Moody's says catastrophe bond spreads have compressed as excess reinsurance capital drives down pricing, with the sharpest declines in remote tail-risk layers. As low-expected-loss deals become uneconomic for investors, capital is moving toward higher-risk cover such as aggregate protections and secondary perils like wildfire and severe convective storm.

California's new AI restrictions raise fresh questions for EPLI

Insurance Business America, quoting Founder Shield (broker view) · Oct 6 · signed into law; effective July 1, 2027
Gov. Gavin Newsom signed SB 947, the "No Robo Bosses Act," on Sept. 30. From July 1, 2027, California employers may not make disciplinary or termination decisions based solely on automated systems, must have a human verify decisions that rely primarily on algorithmic output, and must give affected employees expanded notice. A Founder Shield practice lead advises employers to treat third-party AI tools as their own liability.

Business Insurance · Oct 6 · capacity expected to be finalized in Q4
Amwins launched illumin Specialty, an MGA writing primary general liability and excess liability for complex risks through wholesale brokers only. It will focus on real estate, hospitality, products liability and complex operations, and expects to finalize capacity and accept new business in Q4 2026.

Insurer, coverholder launch US cargo program

Business Insurance · Oct 6 · product launch
Chaucer Group and cargo specialist Indemni launched a U.S. cargo program offering up to $15 million per shipment for land transit risks. It pairs coverage with Indemni's driver verification and shipment tracking to prevent theft and fraudulent pickups. Indemni also gained Lloyd's coverholder status, backed by Chaucer, Aviva, Atrium, Blenheim and Talbot.

Louisiana court reverses comp denial over driver's employment status

Business Insurance · Oct 5 · state appellate ruling
Louisiana's Fourth Circuit Court of Appeal reversed a denial of comp benefits for a delivery driver, finding RNK Construction controlled his work enough to make him an employee despite issuing him a 1099. The court pointed to RNK owning, maintaining and insuring the truck, assigning deliveries, and tracking him by GPS, along with uniforms and discipline policies.

Risk spending rises as threats converge

CIR Magazine, citing the Hiscox Risk Report 2026 (insurer survey) · Oct 6
A Hiscox survey of 800 risk managers found 69% of organizations plan to raise risk management spending over the next year, but only 48% think their frameworks adequately track interconnected risks. Half named coordinating responses across teams and insurance policies as their main obstacle during interconnected disruptions.
