AM Best: Social Inflation Drives Surge in Commercial Auto Liability Claims

Insurance Journal, citing AM Best (rating agency report) · · Oct 23
AM Best found commercial auto liability first-12-month loss payments rose 6.4% a year from 2014 to 2024, against average economic inflation of 2.8%. Claims above typical $1 million auto limits spill into umbrella policies, and umbrella pricing has diverged from general liability pricing since 2018 without returning to its earlier level. AM Best cites Florida's tort reforms as an example of litigation costs being offset, with average paid severity there trending down.

Reinsurers enter January renewals flush as record El Niño builds

Insurance Business America, citing Gallagher Re, Marsh (brokers) and NOAA · · Oct 22 · builds on Wednesday's Gallagher Re loss item
Dedicated reinsurance capital has reached a record of about $688 billion, and Marsh reports US commercial property rates fell 10% in Q3 after a 13% drop in Q2. NOAA puts the odds of a strong to very strong El Niño persisting through early 2027 above 83%. Gallagher Re's analysis since 1980 shows annualized US insured catastrophe losses averaging about $27.7 billion in El Niño conditions, versus $45.8 billion in neutral and $58.8 billion in La Niña conditions.

Kinsale's property retreat signals where the E&S market has turned

Insurance Business America · · Oct 23 · Q3 2026 company results
Kinsale's Q3 gross written premiums fell 4.8% to $462.9 million, with property divisions down 19.3% and commercial property down 26.8%, while casualty grew 0.9%. Net income fell 30% to $98.1 million, mostly because of a $37.6 million pre-tax investment repositioning loss, and the combined ratio was 75.4%. The article cites the WSIA 2026 midyear report as showing property easing while commercial auto, umbrella and general liability continue to harden.

Munich Re raises full-year 2026 profit target as Q3 result well-above consensus

Artemis · · Oct 23 · preliminary results
Munich Re preliminarily reported Q3 2026 net profit of €1.7 billion, against analyst consensus of €1.479 billion. It raised its full-year profit target by €300 million to €6.6 billion. Major losses in its property and casualty reinsurance business were below expectations, and have run below average so far this year.

Laboratory Operator Quest Reports Cyber Breach Affecting Patient Records

Insurance Journal, carrying Reuters · · Oct 22 · investigation ongoing; number affected not disclosed
Quest Diagnostics said an unauthorized party accessed its Quanum LSM system, which some clients use to order tests and look up results, and believes certain patient records were accessed. Quest learned of the incident on Oct. 21, disclosed it in an SEC filing on Oct. 22 and notified federal law enforcement. It says business operations have not been materially disrupted.

$3M default judgment, $10 in damages: insurer's duty-to-defend failure barely stings

Insurance Business America · · Oct 23 · North Carolina Court of Appeals ruling of Oct 21, with a partial dissent
In Eagles v. Discovery Insurance Co., the court held the insurer breached its duty to defend and acted in bad faith after it knew its insured driver had been sued but failed to investigate coverage as its own claims manual required. A $3 million default judgment had been entered against the unrepresented driver in 2021 and was later satisfied by a $1.5 million settlement. The court upheld only $10 in nominal damages, with no treble or punitive damages, plus about $305,000 in attorney fees and costs.

Viewpoint: Generational Shifts, Delayed Projects Are Shaping Builder's Risk

Insurance Journal, written by DUAL North America's builder's risk president (insurer commentary) · · Oct 23
The author says builder's risk submissions are up but quote-to-bind times have stretched well beyond the former 90 days, as tariffs have raised lumber prices by roughly half and builders delay starts. The data center rush has created a five-year lead time for combined cycle turbines, which the author says could make contingent business interruption losses from delays severe. Loss ratios remain high despite the soft market, with arson a major loss driver and some carriers easing security requirements.

Building a Security Strategy for the Quantum Computing Era

Risk Management Magazine (RIMS), written by FTI Consulting advisers (consultant commentary) · · Oct 22
The authors cite NIST's warning that attackers may already be collecting encrypted data to decrypt once quantum computing makes that practical. NIST released its first three finalized post-quantum cryptography standards in August 2024, and cryptographic migrations can take years. They recommend building a cryptographic inventory, budgeting for quantum risk and requiring vendors to provide post-quantum roadmaps before onboarding.

Washington Updates Insurance Claims Handling Rules, Deadlines

Claims Journal (Insurance Journal's sister site) · · Oct 23 · state regulation, in effect since Oct 18
Washington's Insurance Commissioner issued revised claims-handling standards replacing rules not updated since 1978, effective Oct. 18. Insurers must tell policyholders within 30 days whether a claim is accepted or denied, then give updates every 30 days if it is delayed. Insurers also may not deny a claim without an investigation or rely solely on a database for one.
