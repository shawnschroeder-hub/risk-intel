Casualty insurers post $100 billion in cumulative adverse reserve development as analytics lag: Moody's

Risk & Insurance, citing Moody's · Sep 3
Moody's says the US casualty market carries roughly $300bn in annual premium but lacks the analytical tools and shared data standards that property catastrophe risk has. It counts about $35bn of cumulative adverse reserve development in general liability alone over eight years, and roughly $100bn once commercial auto is included. Moody's puts casualty analytics at about where property catastrophe modelling stood in the mid-1990s.

Lloyd's rates fell 6.7% in H1 — nearly twice the pace of last year

Insurance Business America, reporting Lloyd's results · Sep 3
Lloyd's reported H1 2026 gross written premium of £34.7bn, up 6.9%, with a combined ratio of 90.8% against 92.5% a year earlier and profit before tax of £3.5bn, down from £4.2bn. Risk-adjusted rates fell 6.7% in the half, nearly double the 3.5% decline in H1 2025. The underlying combined ratio rose to 84.0% from 82.1%, while the headline ratio benefited from lower major claims (6.8% versus 10.4%).

First half US P/C industry underwriting gain jumps to $31.7BStill relevant

Insurance Journal, citing Verisk and APCIA · Sep 2
US property/casualty insurers posted a net underwriting gain of $31.7bn in the first half of 2026, up from $11.6bn a year earlier, according to a report from Verisk and the APCIA trade association. Net written premium growth slowed to 2.1%.

Blackout risk rises as heat wave stresses largest US electric grids

Insurance Journal, citing the US Department of Energy · Sep 3
The US Department of Energy warned of rising blackout risk as a heat wave stresses PJM Interconnection, which serves 67 million people from Washington DC to Chicago, and MISO across 15 Midwest and Southern states. Energy Secretary Chris Wright issued an emergency order letting PJM direct backup generation to run as a last resort. MISO forecast peak demand near 121 gigawatts against its record of 127.1 GW and raised its operating-reserve requirement.

CAT bond issuance hits record high as reinsurance capital floods marketStill relevant

Risk & Insurance, citing AM Best · Sep 1
AM Best reports catastrophe bond issuance of $17.3bn in the first half of 2026, a record, including $11.3bn in the second quarter alone. Guy Carpenter's global property catastrophe rate-on-line index fell 16% year to date through 1 July, with Florida June renewals down 15% to 20%. AM Best says three consecutive years of strong returns have left capacity providers with more capital than they can prudently deploy.

Nuclear verdicts increase 40.7% in 2025Older but critical

Claims Journal, citing Marathon Strategies (research firm) · Aug 26
Marathon Strategies counted nearly 200 verdicts of $10m or more in 2025, totalling about $25.6bn, up sharply from 2024, including 40 verdicts above $100m and four above $1bn. Products liability accounted for 29 nuclear verdicts totalling $12bn. The firm attributes the rise to corporate mistrust, erosion of tort reform and public desensitisation to large numbers.

California passes three bills tightening residential claims and nonrenewal rulesEnactment not established

Insurance Business America · Sep 3 · legislature-approved
California's Legislature passed three bills tightening residential property claims and nonrenewal rules, now awaiting the Governor's signature. SB 877 requires insurers to provide every estimate version within 15 days; SB 878 sets 40-day claim deadlines and 20% interest on late residential payments; SB 1301 mandates six months' nonrenewal notice and bars cancellation based solely on a claim inquiry.
