Hurricane Isaias Reaches Cat 3 Status as Insurers, Adjusters, Property Owners Prepare

Insurance Journal, citing the National Hurricane Center and Florida OIR data · October 9 · forecast, subject to change

The NHC said Isaias strengthened to a major hurricane on the morning of Oct. 9 with maximum sustained winds of 120 mph, though it is not expected to make landfall at peak intensity. Landfall is forecast for the Pensacola–Florida Panhandle area, with more than six feet of storm surge and up to 15 inches of rain, and significant wind and flood damage possible Friday night. The storm is expected to weaken quickly over Alabama and could be a tropical storm by Saturday afternoon.

02

Isaias puts $71.8 billion in Gulf Coast commercial property in its path

Insurance Business America, citing Moody's exposure data · October 9 · exposure estimate, not a loss estimate

Moody's data show 31,659 commercial properties in Alabama, Florida and Mississippi, with a combined estimated value of $71.8 billion, have a better-than-50% chance of facing winds of at least 50 mph. About 14,945 of them are multi-family buildings valued at roughly $40.2 billion, and Mobile, Pensacola and Biloxi are the most exposed cities. For comparison, Hurricane Sally in 2020 produced about $2 billion in privately insured losses, per Karen Clark & Company.

Hurricane Simon rapidly intensifying, bringing Mexico catastrophe bond back into focus

Artemis, citing the National Hurricane Center · October 9 · forecast; bond not triggered

Pacific Hurricane Simon was a Category 1 storm at 978mb and is forecast to reach winds of up to 145 mph before a Sunday landfall in southwestern or west-central Mexico. Mexico's $175 million IBRD CAR Mexico 2024 (Pacific) parametric cat bond pays out only if central pressure falls to about 937mb or lower inside set coastal zones. 07

California comp report signals pressure for higher employer costs

Business Insurance, citing the WCIRB of California quarterly experience report · October 9

The average charged workers' comp rate in California rose to $1.64 per $100 of payroll in the first half of 2026 from $1.63 in 2025, which the article says could reverse a decade of declines. The WCIRB projects a 127% combined ratio for accident year 2025, the highest in over 20 years, and indemnity claim frequency rose 3.6% in the first half. Cumulative trauma claims have grown from about 18% of indemnity claims in 2022 to over 25%.

09

Wisconsin court upholds wage award over refusal to rehire injured welder

Business Insurance · October 8 · appellate ruling

The Wisconsin Court of Appeals upheld a wage-loss award against Milwaukee metal fabricator Bartelt Custom Automotive, which laid off a welder after his hand injury and did not offer him an open press brake operator job. The court rejected the employer's argument that the worker had to prove he applied and was refused because of his injury. Once the worker shows a workplace injury and denial of rehire, the burden shifts to the employer to show reasonable cause.

10

Divided court revives temp worker's injury suit against concrete company

Business Insurance · October 8 · 2–1 appellate ruling; case remanded

A staffing-agency worker's arm was caught under a cement mixer blade while cleaning it at Johnson Concrete Company's direction, and he sued the manufacturer for negligence. The North Carolina Court of Appeals ruled 2–1 that, without an express or implied hiring contract, workers' comp exclusivity does not bar the suit, because the staffing firm hired, assigned and paid him. The dissent said the company's training and supervision established an implied employment relationship.

South Korean Banks Likely Hacked By China-Based Actor With AI Agent, CrowdStrike Says

Claims Journal, citing Reuters and a CrowdStrike report (security vendor) · October 9 · attribution is CrowdStrike's assessment

At least nine South Korean banks have been reported as targeted in cyberattacks since late September, with Shinhan Bank saying data on about 25,000 customers was compromised. South Korean police have opened a probe.

CIR Magazine · October 9 · approved by members; regulatory approval pending
