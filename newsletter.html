Risk & Insurance · August 2

Why it matters — if that view holds, the soft property market survives this hurricane season. An argument for pressing on rate rather than locking in long-term deals now. Treat it as opinion, not forecast.

08

Three states have now enacted litigation-funding disclosure laws — and Arizona added a court rule

Lockton · Enacted at state level — federal bill still only introduced

Since the start of 2025, Georgia, Mississippi and Tennessee have each enacted legislation requiring disclosure of funding arrangements or funder identities. Separately, the Arizona Supreme Court amended the state's rules of civil procedure to require parties in civil cases to disclose a third-party funder's identity, financial terms and degree of strategic control at the outset of litigation.

Why it matters — status precision matters here. The federal S.3826 is still sitting in the Senate Judiciary Committee, but in these four jurisdictions disclosure is already the rule, and defense counsel can ask today.

Why it matters — this updates the 2024 figures carried in yesterday's edition with a full-year 2025 read. The count is spread across more than half the states, so this is not a venue problem that can be managed by footprint alone.
