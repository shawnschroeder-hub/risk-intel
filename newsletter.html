08

Litigation Funding Transparency Act of 2026 (S.3826) introduced in the Senate

Congress.gov · 119th Congress · Introduced — not law

Sens. Grassley, Tillis, Kennedy and Cornyn would require parties to disclose the identity of any third-party litigation funder — and whether that funder is a foreign state, sovereign wealth fund or foreign-controlled enterprise — in class actions, multidistrict litigation and coordinated proceedings of 100 or more cases. Funding agreements would be filed with the court and all named parties, and funders would be barred from influencing litigation strategy or settlement.

Why it matters — the furthest-reaching federal move yet at the capital behind large-scale civil claims. Status matters: this bill has been introduced, not passed by either chamber.

Why it matters — this is the severity curve casualty pricing is now built on, and the reason US liability rates rose in Q2 while everything else fell.
