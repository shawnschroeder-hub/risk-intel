Lead Stories1–5
01

Reinsurers post near-record returns, but the numbers have a catch

Insurance Business America, citing Gallagher Re (broker research) · September 1

Gallagher Re's 2026 half-year report puts its tracked reinsurer cohort's headline return on equity at 19.9% and the combined ratio at a record-low 85.8%, against 92.1% in H1 2025. Stripping out catastrophe tailwinds and prior-year reserve releases leaves an underlying ROE of 13.8%, down from 15.3% a year earlier. Natural catastrophe losses of $46bn ran 28% below the ten-year average, and that shortfall added 3.4 percentage points to the headline ROE.

02

Insurers must brace for $171 billion in annual cat losses, study finds

Insurance Business America, citing Verisk (catastrophe modeller) · September 1

Verisk's global modelled average annual insured catastrophe loss is now $171bn, of which $117bn is attributed to the United States, against $59bn when the benchmark was first published in 2012. Verisk puts insured losses of at least $477bn at a 1% annual exceedance probability. Severe thunderstorm accounts for 40% of that modelled global average annual loss and tropical cyclone 27%.

03

Off-site storage is becoming a construction placement issue, Arch warns

Insurance Business America, citing Arch Insurance International (insurer research, 44 respondents) · September 1

Arch's inaugural Construction Risk Report, based on a survey of 44 property owners, developers and contractors worldwide, reports 78% seeing greater volatility in material sourcing and 52% naming supply chain disruption a leading risk. Two-thirds increased insurance and risk management spending, and nearly a third reconsidered or relocated projects over geopolitical concerns. Arch underwriters say ordering equipment earlier concentrates theft, fire and natural catastrophe exposure in warehouses, and that storage quality, security and accumulated values should be discussed with insurers at the design stage.

04

Amazon sued by FTC over claims it misled advertisers on pricing

Insurance Journal · September 1 · Allegations only — no findings

The Federal Trade Commission and 22 state attorneys general have sued Amazon in federal court in Seattle, alleging it overcharged roughly 1.2 million advertising customers since 2019 by inflating auction prices without adequate disclosure across its Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display products. The case is docketed as FTC v. Amazon, 26-cv-3097, US District Court for the Western District of Washington. Amazon disputes the claims, says the suit "misunderstands how our auction worked," and states its auctions saved advertisers $8bn over the five years to 2025.

05

Sony and Warner Music sue Anthropic over songs used in AI training

Insurance Journal · September 1 · Allegations only — no findings

The music publishing arms of Sony Music and Warner Music have sued Anthropic in California federal court, alleging it obtained song lyrics and sheet music through torrent downloads to train its Claude models and that the models can reproduce lyrics verbatim. The publishers seek statutory damages of up to $150,000 per work and an order barring use of their works. Anthropic says it will defend itself and maintains that training on copyrighted material is fair use.

06

AI-enabled fraud tops list of emerging claim severity risks, survey finds

Risk & Insurance, citing CorVel (vendor survey of RIMS attendees) · August 26 · Older but critical

In CorVel's survey of 2026 RIMS conference attendees, 32% named AI-enabled fraud and data authenticity as the factor most likely to increase claim severity over the next three to five years. Social inflation was second at 25%, ahead of medical inflation and comorbidities at 17% and economic volatility from supply chain or tariff pressure at 14%. On adoption barriers, 33% cited governance concerns and 23% said they lacked the skills to operationalise AI insights.

07

Former Louisiana attorney charged in Hurricane Ida fraud scheme

Insurance Business America · September 1 · Charged, not convicted

Richard William Huye III, 34, was charged on 28 August with conspiracy to commit wire fraud in connection with Houston law firm McClenny Moseley & Associates. Prosecutors allege the firm sent at least 856 false or misleading letters of representation to insurers over Hurricane Ida claims, with eight Louisiana homeowners named in federal documents. Louisiana regulators fined the firm and related parties $2m in 2023, and the Louisiana Supreme Court suspended Huye from practice in March 2023.

Alternative capital rose 9% in H1 2026 to a record $147bn, Gallagher Re reports

Artemis, citing Gallagher Re (broker research) · September 1

Alternative reinsurance capital reached a record $147bn in the first half of 2026, up 9% since the end of 2025, within total reinsurance capital of $688bn. Catastrophe bond issuance hit a record of nearly $18bn in the half, taking the outstanding market to $65.6bn. Traditional reinsurance capital stands at $541bn, up 23% since the end of 2023.

09

SEADRIF makes over $1 million in payouts to Lao PDR and WFP under parametric policies

Artemis · September 1

The Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Insurance Facility paid $1.14m after flooding in Lao PDR affected more than 260,000 people, passing a 200,000-person threshold — $1m to the government and $137,500 to the World Food Programme. Payouts were completed within five business days, with Gallagher Re acting as calculation agent. Artemis describes the trigger, which uses government-reported impact data aggregated nationally rather than a physical measurement, as among the first of its kind.

Manufacturing & Workplace10–11
10

Calibration costs are reshaping auto physical damage claims

Insurance Business America, citing Enlyte and CCC Intelligent Solutions (vendor reports) and IIHS/HLDI · September 1

Advanced driver assistance system calibration appeared on 34.7% of repair estimates in 2025, up from 12.1% in 2022, at an average cost of $688 per estimate. The reporting cites industry data suggesting more than 60% of vehicles undergoing qualifying repairs may require calibration while only about a third receive it. Average repair cost reached $4,818 with a 23.1% total loss rate in Q4 2025, and IIHS/HLDI analysis puts collision claim severity roughly 10% higher for ADAS-equipped vehicles.

11

Diversified transport and fuel will be key to low carbon freight

CIR Magazine, citing Reuters Events with AXA XL (insurer-sponsored research) · September 1

A Reuters Events report produced with AXA XL evaluates nine alternative fuels and propulsion systems for commercial shipping, which carries roughly 90% of world freight. It finds the readily available options deliver limited emissions reductions while the more promising technologies raise safety, infrastructure, cost and compliance challenges. The report describes technologies advancing at different speeds across maritime, road, rail and air, producing distinct risk profiles by mode.

13

Manchester Airports Group hit by major cyber attack

CIR Magazine · August 27 · Older but critical

A breach discovered on 25 August compromised data belonging to approximately 8.7 million customers of Manchester, London Stansted and East Midlands airports, taken from car park, lounge and Fast Track bookings and in-airport WiFi sign-ups. Email addresses, phone numbers, vehicle registrations and postcodes were exposed; the group said bank and payment details were not accessed and airport operations were unaffected. The group suspended its Manage My Booking service, restricted system access and engaged external cyber security specialists.
