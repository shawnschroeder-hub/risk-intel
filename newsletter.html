14

PFAS exclusions are now standard ISO endorsements on GL, BOP and umbrella forms

Inszone Insurance (broker guidance) · No date shown on source page

ISO specimen forms CG 40 32 05 23 (general liability) and CU 34 54 (umbrella and excess) specifically exclude perfluoroalkyl and polyfluoroalkyl substances, and now appear on Commercial General Liability, Business Owners and Umbrella forms — removing ambiguity and shifting the risk back to the insured. For context, PFAS litigation has already produced more than $11bn in water-system settlements, and recent regulatory and litigation activity has reached secondary manufacturers, textile manufacturers and cosmetics manufacturers.

Why it matters — the most concrete action item in this edition: ask the broker directly whether either endorsement appears on our renewal quotes. Note that this is broker-published guidance and undated, so the form editions should be verified against the actual policies.
