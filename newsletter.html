Hellman & Friedman explores sale of Applied Systems at up to $10 billion, sources say

Insurance Journal · Business Insurance · September 24 · sourced report, not confirmed by the parties

Hellman & Friedman is exploring a sale of insurance agency-management software provider Applied Systems that could value the Chicago-based company at up to $10 billion, with JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs advising, according to sources. 02

Cyber threats the top business concern as AI heightens risk: Travelers Risk Index

Insurance Journal, reporting Travelers' Risk Index (insurer survey) · September 23

Travelers' latest Risk Index found 58% of the 1,202 business decision-makers surveyed cite cyber as their top concern, ranking it first for the fifth time in eight years. Eighty-nine percent use AI in daily operations but only 59% have policies governing employee AI use, and 55% worry specifically about AI-related breaches. Cyber insurance purchase rates reached 70%, the highest since the index became cyber-focused in 2018.

03

AI governance isn't keeping pace with AI adoption, AXA XL warns

Insurance Business America, citing McKinsey and WEF data · September 23

AXA XL warned that AI governance is not keeping pace with AI adoption, citing McKinsey data that 88% of organizations now use AI in at least one business function, up from 78% a year earlier. The World Economic Forum's Global Cybersecurity Outlook 2026 found 94% of respondents expect AI to be the most significant cybersecurity driver this year and 87% believe AI-linked vulnerabilities have grown faster than any other threat type. AXA XL said AI risk exposure increasingly spans multiple insurance lines at once.

04

Beazley broadens cover for AI risks

CIR Magazine · September 24

Beazley added two endorsements to its cyber offering addressing risk from clients' own internal AI use: an AI Voluntary Shutdown solution covering losses from suspending a malfunctioning AI system, and AI Regulatory Defence and Penalties cover for investigations tied to unintentional AI misuse. Both sit within Beazley's existing Full Spectrum Cyber offering.

Washington proposes 4.9% workers comp rate increase

Business Insurance · September 23 · proposed, not finalized

Washington's Department of Labor & Industries proposed a 4.9% increase to workers' comp rates for 2027, citing rising medical and wage-replacement costs. The increase equates to about $1.44 per week per full-time employee, split 75% employer / 25% worker under the state's hours-based premium structure. Washington runs a monopolistic state fund, so payroll growth alone does not raise premium income the way it does under payroll-based systems.

Lockton unveils builders risk for data centers

Business Insurance · September 23

Lockton launched a Builders Risk and Property Program for data center owners, developers, operators and investors, backed by more than $6 billion in builders-risk capacity and $7.5 billion in operational property capacity from a panel of global carriers. The program targets the coverage gap that opens when large data center projects go operational in phases while construction continues on other portions.

07

Summer storms across the Canadian Prairies caused over $1.8bn in insured losses: CatIQ

Artemis, citing CatIQ (catastrophe-data vendor) · September 24

Ten extreme weather events across the Canadian Prairies this summer caused more than $1.8 billion in insured losses, per CatIQ, with a June 9–10 storm across Saskatchewan and Manitoba alone accounting for $900 million. Flooding dominated 2026's damage, a departure from the wind, hail and wildfire losses that typically define Prairie summers. Canada's annual insured losses from extreme weather have risen roughly 320% since the late 1990s/early 2000s.

Markets/coverages: CFC begins affirmative AI for intellectual property; CRC Specialty launches Insurisk Cyber

Insurance Journal · September 24

CFC introduced affirmative AI language for its intellectual property policies, confirming coverage for AI-involved IP disputes — including cases where a business's own AI deployment creates alleged-infringement exposure — without expanding the policy's underlying scope. Separately, CRC Specialty launched Insurisk Cyber, bundling cyber liability, technology E&O and miscellaneous professional liability into one policy backed by A- or better rated carriers, with active threat monitoring included.

09

Marine insurance must shift from "repair to prevention" — IUMI

Insurance Business America · September 23

IUMI Loss Prevention Committee chair Pascal Dubois told the organization's Rotterdam annual conference that marine insurance is shifting from a "repair" to a "prevention" posture, citing Colorado's HB25-1182 linking FAIR Plan wildfire eligibility to mitigation efforts as one example. Dubois said marine insurers still employ far more underwriters and claims handlers than loss-prevention specialists, a balance he expects to shift. IUMI's Loss Prevention and Cargo Committees are also working on ISPM 15 wood-packaging compliance to reduce supply-chain disruption.

60% expect property cat rates to fall, 86% expect T&Cs to loosen: Fitch reinsurance survey

Artemis, reporting a Fitch Ratings survey of reinsurers · September 23

A Fitch survey of reinsurers found 60% expect property catastrophe pricing to fall in 2027, with 20% predicting declines over 10%, and 86% expect terms and conditions to loosen — 58% selectively, 28% broadly. Fewer than 10% of respondents prioritize property/cat risk for capital allocation, with life and health ranking highest instead. Eighty-eight percent of Fitch-rated global reinsurance groups carry a Stable Outlook.
