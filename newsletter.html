Florida tort reform delivers as carriers slash auto rates across the board

Insurance Business America · August 7 · Reform enacted 2023, effects now measurable

Three years after Florida's litigation reforms, carriers are reducing auto rates across the market, with the report framing the price movement as the delayed but measurable effect of the statutory change.

Why it matters — the most encouraging item in this section, and the one to keep in mind whenever tort-reform proposals are dismissed as symbolic. 08

Insurers need not cover a $27 million M&A shareholder settlement, Delaware judge rules

Business Insurance · August 2026 · Delaware Superior Court · D&O bump-up exclusion

A Delaware Superior Court judge ruled that an American International Group unit and two other insurers do not have to reimburse a policyholder's $27m shareholder settlement, on the basis that the settlement effectively increased the purchase price in an acquisition — the classic trigger for a directors and officers "bump-up" exclusion.

Why it matters — the single most actionable item here for a CFO's own risk. Bump-up exclusions are standard in D&O wordings and frequently unexamined until a deal produces a shareholder suit. Delaware governs most large corporate transactions, so the ruling has weight well beyond the parties. Any acquisition on the horizon should trigger a wording review with counsel, specifically on how "bump-up" and "damages" are defined.

Business Insurance · August 2026 · Appellate reversal

An appellate court has reversed a $20m ruling that had gone against an insurer in the assisted living sector.

Why it matters — included as the counterweight to the severity narrative this section usually carries.
