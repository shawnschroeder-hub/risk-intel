01

Microsoft races to fix Outlook in one of its longest outages

Insurance Journal, via Bloomberg · Insurance Business America · November 13 · developing; Microsoft says not a cyberattack

A Microsoft 365 outage that began shortly after noon ET on November 12 was still not fully resolved on November 13, disrupting Outlook, Teams, Copilot and some security and admin tools, with Downdetector complaints from more than 70 countries. Microsoft says a core process on the affected servers became overwhelmed and that the cause was not malicious. Richemont and Workhorse Group postponed earnings releases, and some users could not reach other business apps because their multifactor codes were delivered by email.

02

Zurich North American premiums rise 5% as construction business grows

Business Insurance · November 12 · insurer’s nine-month trading update

Zurich’s North American property/casualty gross written premiums rose 5% to $18.69 billion in the first nine months of 2026, with US construction up 17% on infrastructure and technology-related projects. Commercial rates fell 1% globally, driven by softer large-property pricing, and were flat in North America. Zurich reported that rate declines are slowing in US financial lines and cyber.

03

Wildfires causing $1 billion in damage have jumped in frequency in US

Insurance Journal, via Bloomberg, citing Climate Central (nonprofit research group) · November 13

Climate Central counts 26 billion-dollar US wildfires from 2007 through 2026, against five in the previous 20 years, with inflation-adjusted losses rising to $170 billion from $15.6 billion. California accounts for 23 of the 31 such fires since 1980 and $167 billion of the losses. The costliest is the 2025 Palisades fire in Los Angeles, at $36.3 billion.

OpenAI, Google, Anthropic hit with lawsuit seeking to halt frontier AI development

Insurance Journal, via Reuters · November 13 · lawsuit filed; no ruling

A group of tech-accountability nonprofits including Public Citizen sued OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and SpaceX’s AI division in San Francisco, seeking an order barring further development of advanced AI models. The suit relies on California’s unfair business practices law and also alleges a public nuisance. Meta declined to comment and the other companies did not immediately respond.

05

Cat bond market breadth expands, issuance concentrates in 1% to 3% EL range: Euler ILS Partners

Artemis, citing Euler ILS Partners (fund manager) · November 12

Euler ILS Partners reports the catastrophe bond market up 17.3% year on year after the third quarter, with outstanding deals up 20% to 402. The average coupon fell 9.2% to 7.14% and the average spread fell 21.6% to 5.05%, while the average expected loss rose 12.5% to 2.4%. US hurricane risk makes up about 75% of exposure.

06

ILS seeks growth beyond property cat as investors explore new opportunities

Artemis · November 13 · conference panel remarks

At PwC’s Insurance Summit in Bermuda, Hudson Structured Capital Management’s Michael Millette said capital markets provide about $140 billion of the roughly $500 billion in global catastrophe limits. Panelists said capital markets are getting involved earlier in casualty, cyber and specialty risk, and that the investor base is shifting toward long-term pools such as pension and sovereign wealth funds.

07

GLP-1 spending in workers comp rises nearly 40% annually: NCCI

Business Insurance, citing NCCI · November 12

NCCI reports that workers’ compensation spending on GLP-1 drugs has grown nearly 40% a year since 2021, reaching $1.9 million in 2025. That is still only about 0.02% of the $9.3 billion in medical payments NCCI analyzed. About 40% of claims involving the drugs included a diabetes diagnosis, and NCCI found no meaningful use for weight loss ahead of surgery.

08

Willis names engineer with Pfizer background to lead global risk team

Insurance Business America · November 13 · appointment effective Jan 1, 2027

Willis, a WTW business, named Chris Mulligan global head of risk engineering, leading 200 risk engineers in 30 countries from January 1, 2027. He joined Willis in 2009 after eight years at Pfizer as a process engineer on high-hazard manufacturing sites. His remit is to link site engineering assessments with Willis’s data, analytics and broking so clients can see how exposures interact across operations.

09

Rising costs and cyber threats challenge IT resilience

CIR, citing 11:11 Systems (vendor study) · November 13 · UK survey

An 11:11 Systems study found 89% of UK respondents saw data centre infrastructure costs rise over the past year, by an average of 14%, and 73% had at least one cyber attack or breach. Some 40% said at least some of their backup repositories were affected during an attack. While 91% were investing in or evaluating AI for IT and security operations, only 20% had AI-enhanced backup or disaster recovery.

Intact to merge UK and European specialty lines

Business Insurance, citing Insurance Times · November 12 · takes effect Jan 1, 2027

Intact Insurance plans to combine its UK and European specialty lines into a single London-based operation from January 1, 2027. Intact says the aim is to make it easier to support customers with operations spanning multiple countries. No financial figures were given.

11

Property insurance slump drives Conduit’s premium decline

Business Insurance, citing Reinsurance News · November 12 · company results

Reinsurer Conduit’s gross written premiums fell 4% to $1 billion in the first three quarters of 2026, with property down 10% to $532.9 million and specialty down 3% to $157.9 million. The company said it kept its focus on higher-margin excess-of-loss business in a softening market.

12

Startup specialty insurer Pinion gets green light to acquire Catlin

Insurance Journal · November 12 · regulatory approval; terms undisclosed

Pinion, a Bermuda-incorporated, London-headquartered startup backed by up to $180 million from Barings, received Oklahoma regulatory approval to acquire US excess and surplus lines writer Catlin Specialty Insurance Co. Pinion plans to provide insurance capacity for managing general agents in the US, UK and Europe. Terms were not disclosed.
