01

AI-fueled cyberattacks drive claims surge at Japan insurers

Claims Journal, via Bloomberg · November 20 · insurers’ own figures

Tokio Marine received three times as many cyber claim notifications in October as usual, and Sompo said its October claims ran at 2.5 times its average for the year ended Sept. 30. The article says US insurers reported more than 56,000 cyber claims in 2025, up 20% from a year earlier. Tokio Marine said it may cap payouts or tighten coverage terms depending on a customer’s risk profile.

02

JPMorgan, Visa warn of cyberattack that hit outside vendor

Insurance Journal, via Bloomberg · November 19 · third-party vendor breach; companies’ statements

JPMorgan believes hackers accessed marketing data held by an outside firm, which may include customer names and email addresses, and says the issue is unrelated to its outage earlier in the week. Visa said the incident involved a third-party company in Asia and that its own systems and card numbers were not affected. Mastercard described the exposed data as mostly email addresses.

03

Labor Department makes it easier for companies to classify workers as contractors

Insurance Journal, via Reuters · November 20 · final rule; effective 60 days after formal publication

The Department of Labor’s final rule replaces a Biden-era rule with a test focused on control and opportunity for profit or loss. It takes effect in January, 60 days after formal publication. Trucking, healthcare, retail sales and app-based delivery are named as likely beneficiaries, and companies relying on contractors routinely face misclassification lawsuits.

Cat bond issuance can reach $25bn in 2026, market size to exceed $70bn soon: SCOR IP

Artemis, citing SCOR Investment Partners (asset manager; forecast) · November 19, updated Nov 20 · manager’s outlook

05

NC Farm Bureau sponsoring its third catastrophe bond, $475m Blue Ridge Re 2026-1

Artemis · November 20 · deal in marketing, not yet priced

The North Carolina Farm Bureau is targeting $475 million of per-occurrence, indemnity-trigger cover for North Carolina named storm, severe thunderstorm and winter storm across five tranches. Price guidance ranges from 2.75%–3% on the top Class A notes to 8%–8.5% on the lowest-attaching Class C1 notes. The issue looks set to renew and potentially expand a $400 million bond maturing at year-end.

06

Construction comp claims cost 52% more than average: WCRI

Business Insurance, citing the Workers Compensation Research Institute (research body) · November 19

A WCRI study across 27 states found total payments averaged $47,657 per lost-time construction claim, versus $31,355 across all industries. Construction made up 8% of lost-time claims but 13% of total claim payments. Some 43% of construction claims involved major surgery, against 27% overall.

07

Fortifying against AI-enhanced social engineering attacks

Risk Management Magazine, citing Resilience and Coalition (cyber insurers) and HoxHunt (security vendor) · November 18

Resilience’s 2026 midyear report found 85.3% of incurred cyber losses this year traced to someone trusting a legitimate-looking voice, message or request, up from 17.7% two years ago. Coalition’s 2026 claims report found 71% of funds transfer fraud claims came directly from social engineering. The article notes social engineering endorsement limits are typically $100,000 to $250,000.

08

Aon launches surety program for US digital infrastructure

Insurance Journal, citing Aon (broker) · November 20 · broker product launch

Aon’s new Data Center Surety Program uses insurers as surety for data center developers’ interconnection and power network-upgrade obligations. Aon says it has seen such obligations range from about $5 million to $300 million, many between $20 million and $100 million. The US accounts for more than 90% of data center capacity in the Americas.

09

LA warns of evacuations as atmospheric river hits coast

Claims Journal, via Bloomberg · November 19 · event in progress

Los Angeles County issued evacuation warnings for neighborhoods near the Palisades, Hurst and Sunset burn scars. Downtown San Francisco recorded a record 2.28 inches of rain by 3 p.m., beating a 1918 record. Forecasters said up to 6 inches of rain could fall in coastal mountains and up to 3 feet of snow near Truckee.

Two supertankers hit mines, catch fire in Strait of Hormuz

Business Insurance, citing an Iranian Revolutionary Guard statement via the Associated Press · November 20 · party’s claim, not independently confirmed

Iran’s Revolutionary Guard said two supertankers taking an “unauthorized route” through the strait struck mines and caught fire. It said the ships carried more than 4 million barrels of crude and were crossing the southern section under US military protection.

11

Swedish Club raises rates 4% amid heightened risks

Business Insurance, citing The Swedish Club (marine mutual) · November 20

The Swedish Club announced a 4% general increase for protection and indemnity, freight, demurrage and defense insurance for the 2027–2028 policy year. It cited geopolitical tensions, disruption of key trade routes, shifting trade patterns and more complex shipping regulation.

12

Insured protest losses estimated at $220M

Business Insurance, citing France Assureurs (French insurance federation) via Anadolu Agency · November 20 · Oct 17–18 events

Protests and related violence across France on Oct. 17 and 18 caused more than €200 million ($219 million) in insured damage. Business property accounted for about 95% of the losses. The federation recorded 5,280 claims from the two days of unrest.
